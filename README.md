# ⚡ FundLarper — Validator AI & Startup Pitch Rating Engine
### *Powered by the OnePlus "Never Settle" Design System*

**FundLarper** takes the core validation experience of **ValidatorAI** and elevates it to institutional venture caliber. It evaluates your startup or project idea across both ValidatorAI's signature frameworks (Elevator Pitch Rewrite, Simulated Customer Objection, Simulated VC Pushback, SWOT, Fatal Flaws, 7-Day No-Code Smoke Test) and **8 in-depth investment dimensions**, all wrapped in the iconic **OnePlus Red (`#EB0029`), Obsidian Black, Industrial Grey, and Crisp White** design language.

---

## 🌟 The ValidatorAI Experience (Miles Apart)

### 1. The 2-Sentence Pitch Rewrite (Investor Grade)
Takes your raw, unstructured concept and rewrites it into a razor-sharp, compelling elevator pitch ready to copy and paste into cold emails to angels.

### 2. Simulated Skeptical Customer Objection
Simulates the authentic, blunt resistance of your ideal customer persona (ICP) explaining why they will refuse to switch away from their existing habits or spreadsheets—plus tactical guidance on how to overcome it.

### 3. Simulated VC Diligence Grilling
Models the toughest question a lead venture partner will throw across the diligence table (incumbent risk, defensibility, unit economics), complete with your model defense.

### 4. 4-Quadrant SWOT Matrix
- **Strengths (Internal Advantages):** Unfair operational advantages, software leverage.
- **Weaknesses (Internal Blind Spots):** Integration drag, CAC sensitivity.
- **Opportunities (External Growth):** Market transitions, vertical take-rates.
- **Threats (External Hazards):** Frontier model commoditization, incumbent retaliation.

### 5. Top 3 Fatal Flaws
Calls out the specific structural failure modes that could kill your business if left unaddressed.

### 6. 7-Day No-Code Smoke Test
Provides an exact step-by-step experiment you can run next week for under $100 to prove real customer willingness to pay before writing any code.

### 7. 8-Dimension Strategic Investment Audit & SVG Radar
- **Competitors & Market Alternatives** (Direct, Indirect, Incumbent, White Space)
- **Uniqueness & Moat Architecture** (Switching Costs, Network Effects, IP)
- **Profitability & Unit Economics** (Gross Margins, Pricing Power, Monetization)
- **Market Size & Timing** (TAM/SAM/SOM, Macro Tailwinds)
- **Target Audience & ICP** (Urgency: "Hair-on-Fire" vs "Vitamin", Buyer Persona)
- **Scalability & Leverage** (Software vs Operational Friction)
- **Capital Needed & Runway** (Check Size, Runway, Burn Risk)
- **Longevity & Defensibility** (5-10 Year Outlook, Big Tech & AI Risk)

## 🚀 Running FundLarper

The production server is currently active on **http://localhost:3005**.

To restart or run in development:
```bash
cd FundLarper

# Development
npm run dev

# Or Production
npm run build
npm run start
```

Open [http://localhost:3005](http://localhost:3005) in your browser.
